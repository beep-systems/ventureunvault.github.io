🛠️ An API for word timestamps from speech, 75% within 20ms

Summary
Quebec is an API that provides accurate word timestamps from speech for video editing and talking head footage. It features a custom decoder around an acoustic model and a small CNN trained on Gemini labels to precisely align word boundaries.

Why it’s interesting
It outperforms existing tools like MFA and ElevenLabs Cue on accuracy metrics, with 75% of word boundaries falling within 20ms of hand labels.

Business model
Paid usage priced per hour of audio processed with a minimum top-up.
